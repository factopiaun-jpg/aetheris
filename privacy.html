Aetheris - Privacy Policy

This is a personal, single-user application used to create, organize, and schedule Pinterest pins on my own Pinterest account, and to view my own account's analytics via the official Pinterest API.

This app does not collect, store, or share data belonging to any other Pinterest user. Data accessed via the Pinterest API is used solely to operate this personal tool and is stored locally on the developer's own computer. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
